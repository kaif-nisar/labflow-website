# 6 Month Backlink Strategy

## Month 1
- Submit LabFlow to SaaS directories, startup directories, B2B software directories, and India-focused business listings.
- Build branded profiles on LinkedIn, YouTube, Crunchbase-style directories, and software comparison sites.
- Publish foundational comparison pages and blog posts before outreach.

## Month 2
- Outreach to pathology consultants, lab setup agencies, NABL advisors, and healthcare IT blogs for guest contributions.
- Publish 4 high-intent comparison and workflow articles and use them as linkable assets.

## Month 3
- Secure list-based mentions in “best pathology software”, “best lab software”, and “diagnostic center software” roundups.
- Pitch data-backed posts using operational insights such as billing delays, barcode mismatch reduction, and report turnaround themes.

## Month 4
- Launch YouTube demo snippets and embed them on `/demo`, `/features`, and blog posts.
- Earn links from slide decks, founder posts, and webinar recaps.

## Month 5
- Publish country and city landing pages with regional commentary and use them in local partnership outreach.
- Build links from chamber, startup, and health-tech communities.

## Month 6
- Push PR around feature launches, AI-search readiness, or diagnostic workflow innovation.
- Refresh winning pages and reclaim unlinked mentions for `LabFlow` and branded domain references.
