# 90 Day Ranking Roadmap

## Days 1-15
- Launch crawlable landing pages, metadata, schema, sitemap, robots, and redirects.
- Replace filename-based marketing URLs with clean slugs.
- Add conversion CTAs and sticky demo / WhatsApp buttons.

## Days 16-30
- Publish first 20 blog posts and index them through Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Strengthen internal links between commercial pages and blog posts.
- Add real screenshots, product visuals, and trust proof.

## Days 31-45
- Build backlinks from SaaS and healthcare software directories.
- Publish comparison assets and city pages.
- Improve Core Web Vitals and image delivery.

## Days 46-60
- Add case studies, testimonials, and product walkthrough video embeds.
- Expand pricing, demo, and FAQ pages based on sales objections.
- Launch region pages for UAE, UK, USA, Saudi Arabia, Nepal, and Bangladesh.

## Days 61-75
- Track page-level impressions and optimize titles, intros, and FAQs for CTR.
- Expand topic clusters around barcode, billing, reporting, and LIS workflows.

## Days 76-90
- Consolidate pages earning impressions, improve semantic coverage, and add better proof content.
- Scale outreach, guest posting, and comparison links.
- Improve page sections based on AI search snippets and Search Console questions.
