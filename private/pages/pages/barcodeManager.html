How Pre-Printed Tube Workflow Works

1
Print Roll / A4 Sheet in Advance: Print 50–200 unique stickers and keep them at phlebotomy counters or send rolls to Franchisee collection centers.
2
Stick on Vacutainer Tube & Scan in Booking: Paste sticker on EDTA/Serum tube, place cursor in New Booking → Barcode ID, and scan with a USB scanner — it auto-confirms & jumps to the next tube!
3
Need Patient-Named Stickers Instead? Open All Cases → Print Barcodes or click Submit & Print Barcodes on New Booking to print stickers with Patient Name, Age/Sex & Tests!
